Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

lamvideo is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What data is used

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic account information (such as the display name) from TikTok. It uses them only to upload the owner's videos and captions to the owner's own TikTok account.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. This website does not collect, store or track any visitor data.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
